# AI Usage Framework

This document defines the functional categorization of generative AI tool usage and establishes a 5-tier AI Assistance Level scale for adolescent research.

---

## Part 1: AI Usage Categories

1. **Learning & Tutoring:** Concept explanation, step-by-step guidance, and direct skill acquisition.
2. **Information Search & Retrieval:** Fact-finding, text summarization, and data extraction.
3. **Creativity & Ideation:** Brainstorming, perspective generation, and creative drafting.
4. **Problem Solving & Logic:** Algorithmic logic, debugging, and complex task decomposition.
5. **Decision Making:** Seeking action recommendations, option ranking, and choice guidance.
6. **Emotional Support & Companionship:** Venting, stress reduction, and emotional validation.
7. **Social Interaction & Communication:** Drafting social communications and tone checking.
8. **Entertainment & Casual Usage:** Roleplay, leisure media generation, and gaming.
9. **Content Creation & Drafting:** Full assignment text generation, automated coding, and asset drafting.

---

## Part 2: AI Assistance Levels (Taxonomy)

| Level | Name | Operational Definition | Cognitive Impact Expectation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 0** | **No AI** | 100% human execution without AI intervention. | Baseline human execution. |
| **Level 1** | **AI Assists** | Human drafts; AI performs validation, proofreading, or spellcheck. | High retention, minimal offloading. |
| **Level 2** | **AI Collaborates** | Iterative dialogue; human directs, AI generates options, human evaluates. | **Augmentation zone:** Enhanced ideation and critical engagement. |
| **Level 3** | **AI Performs Major Part** | AI generates core structure (>70%); human makes light edits or formatting. | Early offloading; reduced deep learning. |
| **Level 4** | **AI Replaces Task** | Complete end-to-end task outsourcing. Copy-paste execution without evaluation. | **High Risk zone:** Full cognitive offloading & dependency. |
