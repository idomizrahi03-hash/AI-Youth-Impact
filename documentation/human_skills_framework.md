
# Human Skills Framework

This document outlines the theoretical frameworks and psychometric standards for measuring 7 core human skills in the context of adolescent AI usage.

## 1. Critical Thinking
- **Definition:** Objective analysis, argument evaluation, and bias detection.
- **Validated Scale:** California Critical Thinking Skills Test (CCTST) / Watson-Glaser.
- **AI Context:** Evaluating hallucination rates, cross-checking AI outputs.

## 2. Creativity
- **Definition:** Generating original, useful ideas and non-standard problem-solving.
- **Validated Scale:** Torrance Tests of Creative Thinking (TTCT).
- **AI Context:** Ideation partnership vs. automated output generation.

## 3. Problem Solving
- **Definition:** Structured problem identification, decomposition, and solution selection.
- **Validated Scale:** PISA Problem Solving Framework (OECD).
- **AI Context:** Using AI as a step-by-step tutor vs. solution getter.

## 4. Decision Making
- **Definition:** Evaluating alternatives and risks to arrive at an autonomous choice.
- **Validated Scale:** General Decision Making Style (GDMS).
- **AI Context:** Human choice autonomy vs. decision outsourcing.

## 5. Communication
- **Definition:** Clear, tailored, and authentic expression in writing and speech.
- **Validated Scale:** AACE Core Communication Competencies.
- **AI Context:** Refinement and prompting vs. loss of personal voice.

## 6. Social Interaction
- **Definition:** Building interpersonal relationships, empathy, and collaboration.
- **Validated Scale:** Social Skills Improvement System (SSIS).
- **AI Context:** Human collaboration vs. chatbot social substitution.

## 7. Metacognition & Independent Learning
- **Definition:** Self-monitoring, planning, and evaluation of one's learning processes.
- **Validated Scale:** Metacognitive Awareness Inventory (MAI).
- **AI Context:** Self-directed clarification vs. bypassing the learning curve.
